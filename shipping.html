Delivery & Shipping Protocol

ENTITY: RUPAMDAS.IN
COMMERCIAL NODE: GOLGIX

01.0 Digital Infrastructural Delivery

Upon successful cryptographic verification of a transaction via Zoho Payments, digital assets, PDFs, or API access tokens are deployed instantly via automated server routing. Access credentials or download links will be transmitted to the user's primary email address (the Dendrite terminal) within 5 operational minutes.

02.0 Physical Fulfillment (Golgix Logistics)

For physical items routed through the Golgix terminal, dispatch occurs within 48 to 72 operational hours post-transaction. Standard delivery timelines across the Indian jurisdiction range from 5 to 7 business days. Tracking coordinates will be transmitted to your terminal once the asset leaves our facility.

03.0 Network & Carrier Interruptions

If digital delivery fails due to ISP routing issues, or if a physical asset is delayed due to unforeseen courier anomalies, users are instructed to initiate a transmission to our administrative terminal for manual provision or tracking updates.
