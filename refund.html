Refund & Cancellation Protocol

ENTITY: RUPAMDAS.IN
COMMERCIAL NODE: GOLGIX

01.0 Digital Asset & Service Delivery

Due to the digital and computational nature of the utilities, algorithms, and biological architecture provided by this ecosystem, all digital transactions processed through the Golgix node are considered final upon execution. We do not issue refunds for server processing time, digital downloads, or API access tokens.

02.0 Physical Asset Fulfillment (Golgix)

For physical hardware or biological utility kits procured via Golgix, users possess the sovereignty to request a return within 7 operational days of delivery. Assets must be uncompromised, in original packaging, and free of biological contamination. To initiate a reverse-logistics request, transmit your order coordinates to our administrative terminal.

03.0 Subscription Cancellations

If you have subscribed to a recurring computational service, you may cancel your subscription at any time via your user dashboard. Cancellations will halt future billing cycles, but previously processed transactions will not be retroactively refunded.

04.0 Financial Anomalies & Gateway Resolutions

If a duplicate transaction occurs due to a network anomaly or a routing failure via our payment processor (e.g., Zoho Payments), a manual review will be conducted. Verified anomalies will be refunded to the original cryptographic payment method within 5 to 10 operational days.

05.0 Authorized Refund Mechanics & Deductions

In the event a refund protocol is authorized and initiated, the final disbursed amount will be subject to specific algorithmic deductions. A baseline service charge of 5% may be withheld, contingent upon the extent of the computational or physical services already utilized prior to the request. Furthermore, all third-party payment gateway routing fees incurred during both the initial transaction and the reverse transfer (to and fro) are strictly non-refundable and will be deducted from the final settlement balance.
