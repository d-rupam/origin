For physical hardware or biological utility kits procured via Golgix, users possess the sovereignty to request a return within 7 operational days of delivery. 03.0

Subscription Cancellations

If you have subscribed to a recurring computational service, you may cancel your subscription at any time via your user dashboard. Cancellations will halt future billing cycles, but previously processed transactions will not be retroactively refunded.

04.0

Financial Anomalies & Gateway Resolutions

If a duplicate transaction occurs due to a network anomaly or a routing failure via our payment processor (e.g., Zoho Payments), a manual review will be conducted. Verified anomalies will be refunded to the original cryptographic payment method within 5 to 10 operational days.
